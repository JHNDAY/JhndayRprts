How the 6.5 factor was measured: at a steady console reading of 6, the pedals made 13 revolutions in 20 seconds, the same in three trials. That's 39 RPM, and 39 ÷ 6 = 6.5. Because the count is in whole revolutions, the factor may be off by up to about 5%.
