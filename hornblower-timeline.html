Captain Hornblower

A Chronicle of Naval Service, 1794–1823

The life and trials of Horatio Hornblower through the Napoleonic Wars

Mr. Midshipman Hornblower
1794
⚓ Naval Operations

Seventeen-year-old midshipman aboard HMS Justinien, then transferred to frigate HMS Indefatigable under Captain Pellew. Engages in duels, fireship attacks, cutting-out expeditions, and endures Spanish captivity.

♡ Personal Transformation

Horatio lodges at Mrs. Mason's boarding house in Portsmouth, where he meets Maria Mason—a simple, affectionate young woman who quietly adores him. The awkward, seasick youth matures into a capable officer with a sharp mathematical mind.

Lieutenant Hornblower
1801–1802
⚓ Naval Operations

Serves aboard the 74-gun HMS Renown under increasingly paranoid Captain Sawyer. Brilliant tactical operations in the West Indies and suppression of a Spanish prisoner revolt earn Hornblower his first independent command.

♡ Bonds & Obligation

The origin of lifelong brotherhood between Hornblower and Captain William Bush. Desperate and half-pay in Portsmouth, Horatio proposes to Maria out of financial necessity and gentlemanly obligation—knowing he feels only pity, not romantic passion. Bush serves as his best man.

Hornblower and the Hotspur
1803
⚓ Naval Operations

Takes command of the small sloop HMS Hotspur to scout the rocky French coast off Brest under Admiral Cornwallis. Dangerous blockading operations and shore raids help thwart Napoleon's invasion plans.

♡ Matrimony & Doubt

Hornblower and Maria marry; William Bush serves as first lieutenant aboard Hotspur. While Maria is fiercely devoted, Horatio is racked by guilt over his inability to return her passion. Maria gives birth to their son, also named Horatio, while he is at sea.

Hornblower During the Crisis
1805
⚓ Naval Operations

Entrusted with a covert intelligence mission to deliver forged French naval orders off Spain, influencing enemy fleet positioning for Nelson's victory at Trafalgar. (Unfinished work with narrative notes and short stories.)

♡ Domestic Anchorhold

Maria remains in England caring for young Horatio and pregnant with their second child, serving as a steady domestic anchor while Horatio navigates high-stakes naval politics.

Hornblower and the Atropos
1805–1806
⚓ Naval Operations

Commands the 22-gun HMS Atropos, organizing Admiral Nelson's funeral procession along the Thames before sailing to the Mediterranean to salvage sunken silver treasure from an Ottoman bay.

♡ Tragedy & Tenderness

Personal catastrophe strikes: a smallpox epidemic in Portsmouth kills both of Hornblower's young children, Horatio and little Maria. He returns to find Maria utterly broken. Shared sorrow draws them into a tender, melancholy closeness for the first time.

The Happy Return (Beat to Quarters)
1808
⚓ Naval Operations

Commands HMS Lydia on a secret mission to Central America to aid a rebel warlord against Spain—only to learn mid-mission Spain has allied with Britain. Must track down and destroy the very Spanish warship he had provided to the rebels.

♡ Forbidden Attraction

Tasked with escorting Lady Barbara Wellesley—aristocratic sister of the future Duke of Wellington—back to England. Despite Horatio's marriage and their vast social gulf, an intense romantic and intellectual attraction ignites between them. William Bush observes the unexpressed tension with protective loyalty. Bound by honor, they restrain their feelings, but the encounter permanently alters Horatio's inner life.

A Ship of the Line
1810
⚓ Naval Operations

Commands HMS Sutherland (74 guns) conducting a relentless guerrilla campaign against French coastal positions along Catalonia. Overwhelmed by four enemy ships of the line and forced to surrender.

♡ Loss & Captivity

Lady Barbara has married Rear-Admiral Sir Percy Leighton. Hornblower struggles with jealousy and missing Maria, who is pregnant again. Bush loses his foot to a French cannonball during the final battle and is captured with Hornblower. Unknown to Horatio while imprisoned, Maria dies in childbirth, delivering their son Richard.

Flying Colours
1810–1811
⚓ Naval Operations

Imprisoned in France facing execution, Hornblower and amputee Bush orchestrate a daring winter escape with a wounded coxswain. Hide at a French estate, hijack a French vessel, recapture a British prize ship, and return to England in triumph.

♡ Reunion & Liberation

Hornblower learns of Maria's death and discovers that Lady Barbara has taken in baby Richard. Admiral Leighton dies shortly after. Free at last, Horatio and Barbara marry, elevating Hornblower into high society. Bush is promoted to Commander for his heroism during the escape.

The Commodore (Commodore Hornblower)
1812
⚓ Naval Operations

Promoted to Commodore, commands a Baltic squadron to keep Russia and Sweden in the war against Napoleon, playing a vital role in the defense of Riga during the French invasion.

♡ Partnership & Trust

Hornblower selects Bush as captain of his flagship HMS Nonsuch. At Smallbridge estate, Lady Barbara uses her political connections to ease Horatio's chronic self-doubt. Their marriage evolves into deep affection, while Bush remains his most trusted confidant on the quarterdeck.

Lord Hornblower
1814–1815
⚓ Naval Operations

Suppresses a Royal Navy mutiny off France, supports a Royalist revolt in Bordeaux, and is elevated to the peerage as Baron Hornblower of Smallbridge. During Napoleon's Hundred Days, commands guerrilla resistance in the Loire Valley.

♡ Devastating Loss

The most shattering blow: Captain William Bush is killed during a night attack on French ammunition barges at Caudebec. Hornblower is shattered by the sudden loss of his truest friend. Later, reunited with Marie de Graçay, she is killed by Imperial troops. Saved from a French firing squad by news of Waterloo, he returns to Lady Barbara, his steadfast emotional anchor.

Hornblower in the West Indies
1821–1823
⚓ Naval Operations

As Commander-in-Chief of the West Indies station, navigates post-Napoleonic peace: suppressing pirate fleets, intercepting illegal slave ships, and handling political unrest in South America.

♡ Enduring Love

Lady Barbara joins Horatio in Jamaica. Their relationship has settled into a mature, deeply loving companionship. In a dramatic climax, Barbara survives a violent Caribbean hurricane at sea alongside Horatio, solidifying their shared lifetime of resilience.
