Life Fitness Track Console

Complete Owner's Manual & Operating Guide for C1 & C3 Lifecycle Exercise Bikes

🚴 Quick Start Guide

Get started with your Life Fitness Lifecycle in 5 easy steps.

Step 1: Power On the Console

Plug the power adapter into the wall outlet
The console will light up automatically

Step 2: Select Your Workout

Press the WORKOUT SELECTION BUTTON to browse workouts
Choose from: Classic, HeartSync, Advanced, Goal, or Custom workouts
Press ENTER to select

Step 3: Adjust Resistance (The Answer You're Looking For!)

How to Change Resistance Level During Workout:

Use the LEFT/RIGHT arrow buttons on the console

Resistance Range: Levels 1-20

Level 1 = Easiest | Level 20 = Hardest

Changes take effect immediately as you pedal

Step 4: Start Your Workout

Follow console prompts to enter time, goal, or other settings
Press ENTER/START to begin

Step 5: Monitor Your Stats

Watch the console displays for: Heart Rate, Distance, Calories, Time
Adjust resistance anytime with LEFT/RIGHT arrows

⚡ Resistance Adjustment Cheat Sheet

During Workout:

Increase Resistance: Press RIGHT arrow
Decrease Resistance: Press LEFT arrow
Speed: Changes take effect immediately

Resistance Types:

Brake Levels 1-20: Manual control (Manual workouts)
Difficulty 1-20: Auto-adjusting (Random, Hill, Sports Training)
Resistance Level Intensity Best For
1-5 Very Easy Warm-up, recovery
6-10 Easy Steady cardio
11-15 Moderate Fat burn, endurance
16-20 Hard Strength, intervals

❤️ Heart Rate Setup

Using the Telemetry Chest Strap (Recommended)

This provides the most accurate heart rate reading for heart-based workouts.

Moisten the two electrodes on the underside of the strap
Secure high under your chest muscles
Keep it snug but comfortable for normal breathing
Consoles display your live heart rate within seconds

Using Contact Heart Rate (Hand Sensors)

Available but less accurate than chest strap.

Grasp the sensors on the handlebars firmly
Keep your hands still
Wait 15-20 seconds for heart rate to appear
Dry hands if needed for better contact

Main Display Areas

Top-Left: Level (1-20) - Your current resistance

Top-Center: Time - Workout countdown

Top-Right: Speed (mph/kmh)

Middle: RPM, Distance, Heart Rate, Calories

Bottom: Workout Profile Graph - Visual representation of your workout intensity over time

Console Buttons & Controls

Create Custom Workouts

Design up to 3 personalized workouts per user profile.

How to Create:

1. Press USER PROFILES and select a profile
2. Hold USER PROFILES for 3 seconds
3. Scroll to "Custom Workout" and press ENTER
4. Choose MANUAL or HEART RATE type
5. Set workout time (1-99 minutes)
6. Design each of 24 intervals
7. Press ENTER to save

User Profiles

What Gets Saved:

Custom name

Workout history and goals

How to Log In:

Press USER PROFILES button
Select profile 1, 2, 3, or 4
Press ENTER

Settings Menu

Troubleshooting & Support

No Power?

Check power cord is fully plugged in
Press ENERGY SAVER button
Try unplugging and re-plugging

Heart Rate Not Working?

Check WIRELESS HR is ON in Settings
Wet chest strap electrodes
Move away from electrical interference

Resistance Not Changing?

Ensure LEFT/RIGHT arrows are working
Power cycle the console

Unlike bikes where resistance increases with speed, SCIFIT's electromagnetic resistance system allows you to:

SportsArt ECO-NATURAL Console

Complete Console Operating Guide for Upright Exercise Bikes (C574U/C576U Models)

🚴 Quick Start Guide

Get started on your SportsArt ECO-NATURAL bike in 5 easy steps.

Step 1: Power On the Console

Press the POWER button (bottom right of console)
The display will illuminate and show the startup screen
ECO-NATURAL bikes are self-powered, so no external power needed

Step 2: Select a Workout Program

Browse program options: Manual, Random, Interval, Plateau, Fat Burn, Track, Fitness Test, or Heart Rate
Press ENTER to select your program

Step 3: Adjust Resistance (Key Control!)

How to Change Resistance:

Use the GREEN RESISTANCE + and - buttons

Resistance Range: 40 levels (ECO-NATURAL models)

Press + to increase resistance (harder pedaling)

Press - to decrease resistance (easier pedaling)

Changes take effect immediately

Step 4: Set Your Workout Parameters

Enter time, distance goal, or heart rate target (depending on program)
Use arrow buttons to adjust numeric values
Press ENTER to confirm

Step 5: Begin Your Workout

Press START (green button) to begin
Monitor display for: Time, Distance, Calories, Speed, Heart Rate
Adjust resistance anytime with + / - buttons

⚡ Console Features at a Glance

Display Zones:

• GOAL
• WEIGHT LOSS ZONE (65-75% max HR)
• HEART RATE
• CARDIO ZONE (75-85% max HR)

Key Metrics:

• TIME - Workout duration
• DISTANCE - Miles traveled
• CALORIES - Estimated burn
• SPEED - Current MPH/KPH
• RPM - Pedal revolutions/min
• HEART RATE - Live BPM

Console Layout & Components

Main Display Screen

The large central display shows your current workout metrics in real-time. Different workout programs display different information, but always include TIME and current RESISTANCE LEVEL.

Left Side Display Panel

GOAL Button: Shows workout objectives (distance, time, calories)
Program Selection Icons: Visual indicators for available programs

Right Side Display Panel

Resistance Level Display: Shows current resistance (1-40)
Heart Rate Zone Indicators: Visual guides for fat burn and cardio zones
Numeric Keypad: Numbers 1-9 and 0 for entering values

Bottom Control Area

Program selection buttons (Manual, Random, etc.)
Resistance adjustment buttons (+ and -)
Start/Stop/Enter buttons
Power button (bottom right)

Self-Powered Technology

Your SportsArt ECO-NATURAL console is powered by your pedaling action. As you ride, you generate the electricity to run the console display. No external power outlet required!

Console Button Guide

Control Buttons

POWER (Bottom Right)
Turns console on and off. Press once to power on, press and hold for 3 seconds to shut down.
START (Green Button)
Begins your workout immediately after program and settings are selected.
PAUSE/RESUME
Temporarily pauses your workout. Press again to resume from where you left off.
STOP (Red Button)
Ends your workout immediately and returns to main menu. Data is saved.
ENTER (Yellow Button)
Confirms selections and navigates through menu options. Essential for program setup.

Arrow Navigation Buttons

UP / DOWN Arrows
Scroll through program options and menu items. Navigate up and down in lists.
LEFT / RIGHT Arrows
Adjust numeric values (time, distance, intensity). Left decreases, right increases.

Resistance Control

RESISTANCE + (Green Plus Button)
Increases resistance level during workout. Pedaling becomes harder. Works anytime during exercise.
RESISTANCE - (Green Minus Button)
Decreases resistance level during workout. Pedaling becomes easier. Works anytime during exercise.

Numeric Keypad (1-9, 0)

Use to enter numeric values for workouts (time, distance goals, age, weight). Press numbers then ENTER to confirm.

Display Windows & Metrics

What Each Display Shows

TIME

Elapsed time during your current workout, displayed in minutes:seconds format (MM:SS). Shows countdown during programmed workouts.

DISTANCE

Total miles (or kilometers, if metric is selected) traveled since start of workout. Updates continuously as you pedal.

CALORIES

Estimated calories burned during current workout. Based on your weight, intensity, and duration. Total accumulates throughout workout.

SPEED

Current pedaling speed in MPH (miles per hour) or KPH (kilometers per hour). Updates with each pedal stroke.

RPM

Revolutions Per Minute - how many times you complete a full pedal cycle per minute. Good indicator of cadence/pace.

HEART RATE

Your live heart rate in beats per minute (BPM). Requires holding contact HR sensors on handlebars or wearing Bluetooth chest strap. Updates every few seconds.

RESISTANCE LEVEL

Current resistance setting (1-40). Higher numbers = harder pedaling. Displays prominently so you always know your current level.

GOAL DISPLAY ZONE

Shows your target fitness zone: WEIGHT LOSS (65-75% max HR) or CARDIO (75-85% max HR). Helps you stay in optimal training zone.

Wear Bluetooth-enabled heart rate strap
Pair with console before workout

No need to hold sensors - hands free!
